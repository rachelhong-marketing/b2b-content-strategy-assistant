# How a made-up regional bank cut duplicate customer records by 52% in one quarter

A made-up regional bank had the same customer in four systems and reports that did not match. After merging records with Fernhill Software, duplicates fell by 52% in one quarter and month-end reporting took 3 days instead of 5.

## The challenge

The same customer appeared in the customer database, the customer relationship management (CRM) system and billing. Each team counted customers differently, so month-end reports did not match.

## What they did

They connected the three systems, reviewed the matches the platform suggested, and merged them in batches. Then they turned on monitoring so new duplicates are flagged within a day.

## The results

Duplicate customer records fell by 52% in one quarter. Month-end reporting took 3 days instead of 5 after the merge.
